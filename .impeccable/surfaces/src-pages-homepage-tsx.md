## Direction contract

THESIS: Mexican lotería executed as luxury stationery: every feature is a collectible card, the story goes "carta por carta" and closes with "¡Lotería!". Refuses the toy-bright lotería and the category's couple-photo/script default.

OWN-WORLD: Day paper mode (ivory #F5EFE4 with cotton grain, surfaces #FBF7F0, text #1A1614, secondary #5C524A, gold #A8842F) and night salon mode (#1A1614 with faint grain, surfaces #241F1C, text #F5EFE4, secondary #B8AFA3, gold #C9A45C). Cards always ivory paper, thin black edge, champagne foil inner frame, desaturated inks (carmín #A3262A, azul #1F3A6B, verde #2F5D46, mostaza #D9A62E), ±2° hand-placed tilt, warm soft shadow by day, deep shadow plus 25% gold edge by night. Bodoni Moda display with gold italic accents; Hanken Grotesk UI. Carmín primary button in both modes.

STORY: The visitor sees four cards dealt one by one and understands invitation, RSVP, pass and table for one payment; the RSVP card prints in ink until the family confirms. The tabla shows what else is included; the four templates, including Lotería, are cards; plans are cards; the close is a full tabla and "¡Lotería!". They tap "Diseña gratis".

Right: four cards in one row dealt one by one, gold "now" marker. Phones: scroll-snap carousel with the next card peeking and dots; headline and button above the fold.
